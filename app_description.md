
# Startup Idea Report: AI Weight Loss Companion

**Market:** Iran  
**Business Type:** B2C HealthTech / AI Application  
**Target Users:** People using prescription weight-loss medications  
**Project Stage:** Idea Validation / MVP Planning

### 1. The Idea

Build a mobile-first application that helps people manage their weight-loss journey while using medications such as Ozempic, Mounjaro, or similar treatments.

The goal is **not just to remind users about injections**, but to become their daily personal companion.

The application helps users track their treatment, nutrition, weight, symptoms, and overall progress.

### 2. Existing Competitors

We identified several products worth studying:

| Product | Main Features |
|---|---|
| **Shotsy** | Injection tracking, medication logs, symptoms, weight tracking |
| **MeAgain** | Medication tracking, AI food logging, protein, fiber, hydration |
| **Simple** | AI coaching, nutrition tracking, healthy habits |
| **Karafs (Iran)** | Weight management, medication tracking, injection reminders |

**Our opportunity:** Create a simple, Persian-first experience designed specifically for Iranian users receiving medical weight-loss treatment.

However, being available in Persian is not enough. We need a better user experience and a clear reason for users to pay.

### 3. Core Application Features

**A. Today Dashboard**

The main screen displays:

- Current weight and overall progress
- Days since treatment started
- Upcoming medication reminders
- Daily nutrition summary
- Quick actions to log food, weight, and symptoms

**B. Medication Tracker**

- Save doctor-prescribed medication information
- Track injections and medication history
- Set reminders based on prescribed schedules
- Record injection locations

The application does not recommend medication doses or replace medical advice.

**C. AI Nutrition Assistant**

- Take photos of meals
- Identify Iranian dishes using AI
- Estimate calories, protein, and fiber
- Allow users to correct portion sizes
- Track daily nutrition goals

**D. Symptoms Tracker**

- Record nausea, fatigue, constipation, and other symptoms
- Track symptom severity
- Display symptoms alongside medication history
- Generate useful information for medical appointments

**E. Progress Dashboard**

- Weight tracking and charts
- Body measurements
- Progress photos
- Weekly summaries
- Downloadable reports for doctors

### 4. Future Features

After validating the MVP, we could add:

- Personalized AI insights based on logged data
- AI-powered nutrition coaching
- Advanced weekly reports
- Doctor and clinic dashboards
- Optional sharing of health data with doctors
- Long-term weight maintenance tools

Medical recommendations must remain under professional supervision.

### 5. Suggested Technology Stack

| Component | Technology |
|---|---|
| Frontend | Next.js (Mobile-first PWA) |
| Backend | Python + Django REST Framework |
| Database | PostgreSQL |
| Background Tasks | Celery + Redis |
| AI | Vision models + LLM APIs |
| Authentication | Phone OTP |
| Hosting | VPS / Docker |

**Development approach:** Start with a simple monolithic backend. Avoid unnecessary microservices or complex AI agents.

### 6. MVP Scope

The first version should focus on five features:

1. User onboarding and medication information
2. Medication and injection tracking
3. Weight tracking
4. Daily symptom logging
5. Simple progress dashboard and doctor reports

Advanced AI nutrition features can come later.

### 7. Business Model

**Freemium subscription model**

Free version:

- Medication reminders
- Basic weight tracking
- Symptom tracking

Premium version:

- AI food analysis
- Advanced insights
- Nutrition tracking
- Detailed progress reports

Potential subscription pricing to test in Iran: **99,000–199,000 toman per month**.

These are experimental prices, not validated market rates.

A second revenue opportunity could be monthly subscriptions for clinics and doctors.

### 8. Marketing and Distribution

Our initial acquisition channels could include:

- Partnerships with weight-loss clinics
- Recommendations from doctors
- Instagram educational content
- Cafe Bazaar and Myket
- Partnerships with nutrition specialists

**Initial strategy:** Partner with five doctors and recruit approximately 100 early users.

### 9. Main Risks

Before investing heavily, we must validate:

- Do patients actually need a dedicated application?
- Will users consistently track their progress?
- Are users willing to pay for premium features?
- Can we acquire customers at a reasonable cost?
- Can we comply with Iranian healthcare and privacy regulations?

We should interview at least 15–20 potential users before building the full product.

### 10. Product Roadmap

**Phase 1: Research**  
Understand patient problems, analyze competitors, and validate demand.

**Phase 2: MVP**  
Build a simple mobile-first application with medication, weight, and symptom tracking.

**Phase 3: User Testing**  
Test with real patients and measure engagement and retention.

**Phase 4: AI Features**  
Introduce AI nutrition analysis and personalized insights.

**Phase 5: Monetization**  
Test subscriptions and potential clinic partnerships.

---

### Final Product Vision

**An AI-powered personal companion for people undergoing medical weight-loss treatment in Iran.**

Our competitive advantage should be the combination of:

- Simple and beautiful user experience
- Persian language and Iranian food support
- Medication and health tracking
- AI-powered nutrition insights
- Better communication between patients and doctors

**Key principle:** Validate the problem first, build a small MVP, measure real user behavior, and expand only when there is evidence of demand.

**Current status:** Promising hypothesis, but market demand, willingness to pay, and product-market fit remain unvalidated.
